# HYPOTHESIS: T-00041

## Observed defect
Under the staged Ottawa deck (`audit/runs/issue-100-n100e-deck/runottawa`, `runtime` field-6 `max_nonlinear_iterations = 100`), Zig enforces `hard_max_iterations = 100` as a global hard ceiling across all process budgets. Specifically, `Limits.fromSceneOptions` clamps `solute_reaction_max_iterations = @min(hard_max_iterations, 100)` and `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations = @min(hard_max_iterations, 1000)`. In legacy Fortran, there is no runtime record field 6 or hard max cap (`reads.f:126` reads only `NPX,NPY,JOUT,IOUT,KOUT,ICLM`); legacy sets `MRXN = 60` in `solute.f:111` for hourly reaction and `MRXN = 1000` in `starte.f:101` for initial equilibration. Consequently, under the staged deck, Zig forces both SOLUTE and STARTE budgets to exactly 100 iterations. For STARTE, this imposes a 10x budget reduction (from legacy 1000 down to 100), whereas for SOLUTE, it limits the solver to 100 (which is larger than legacy 60 but exhausted at hour 2,589 in issue-015).

## Evidence
- `ecosys-ng/src/core/iteration_control.zig:131-132`:
  `.solute_reaction_max_iterations = @min(hard_max_iterations, 100)`
  `.initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations = @min(hard_max_iterations, 1000)`
- `audit/runs/issue-100-n100e-deck/runottawa:6` and `ecosys-ng/src/validation/testdata/ottawa/runottawa:6`:
  `runtime,4,1,1e-8,1e-11,100,0.5` sets `max_nonlinear_iterations = 100`.
- `f77src/solute.f:111`:
  `PARAMETER (MRXN=60, ...)`
- `f77src/starte.f:101`:
  `PARAMETER (..., MRXN=1000)`
- `f77src/reads.f:126`:
  `READ(4,*)NPX,NPY,JOUT,IOUT,KOUT,ICLM` (no runtime field 6, no global hard max cap).
- T-00039 root cause packet (`.agent/results/T-00039.md`).

## Proposed mechanism
In Zig, `Limits.fromSceneOptions` applies `hard_max_iterations` to clamp all solver budgets. When the staged deck supplies `max_nonlinear_iterations = 100`:
- `solute_reaction_max_iterations` becomes `@min(100, 100) = 100`.
- `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations` becomes `@min(100, 1000) = 100`.
In legacy Fortran, `solute.f:111` allocates a fixed `MRXN=60` iterations per hour, and `starte.f:101` allocates a fixed `MRXN=1000` iterations for startup equilibration. Legacy options input never restricts or bounds `MRXN`. Clamping STARTE to 100 is an artificial restriction introduced by Zig's global runtime ceiling that deprives STARTE of 90% of its legacy budget, while clamping SOLUTE to 100 binds the hourly reaction solver to the deck's runtime field 6 rather than the legacy specification.

## Proposed change
Add targeted unit tests in `ecosys-ng/src/core/iteration_control.zig` that:
1. Instantiate `SceneOptions` matching the staged Ottawa deck (`water_heat_solute_iteration_limit = 20`, `gas_iterations_per_water_heat_solute_iteration = 4`).
2. Validate that under the staged deck runtime setting `hard_max_iterations = 100`, `Limits.fromSceneOptions` resolves `solute_reaction_max_iterations = 100` and clamps `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations = 100`.
3. Validate that under legacy MRXN values (`solute.f:111` MRXN=60, `starte.f:101` MRXN=1000), if `hard_max_iterations` were not binding (e.g. 1000), `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations` would be 1000 while `solute_reaction_max_iterations` remains capped at 100.
4. Validate that mutating `hard_max_iterations` directly governs the STARTE budget (e.g. at 60, both are 60; at 100, both are 100; at 200, solute is 100 and initial_solute is 200), demonstrating that the staged deck hard cap directly dictates the SOLUTE and STARTE limits in Zig.

## Prediction
The test assertions will pass:
- Under staged deck options with `hard_max_iterations = 100`:
  `limits.solute_reaction_max_iterations == 100` (vs legacy `solute.f:111` MRXN=60)
  `limits.initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations == 100` (clamped from legacy `starte.f:101` MRXN=1000 down to 100).
- Under mutated caps:
  `hard_max = 60` -> `solute_reaction_max_iterations == 60`, `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations == 60`.
  `hard_max = 200` -> `solute_reaction_max_iterations == 100`, `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations == 200`.
  `hard_max = 1000` -> `solute_reaction_max_iterations == 100`, `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations == 1000`.

## Falsification condition
The test fails or either SOLUTE or STARTE iteration limits under `Limits.fromSceneOptions` with `hard_max_iterations = 100` do not evaluate to 100, or `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations` is not clamped by `hard_max_iterations`.
