# HYPOTHESIS: T-00046

## Observed defect
In `ecosys-ng/src/core/iteration_control.zig:131-132`, `Limits.fromSceneOptions` clamps `solute_reaction_max_iterations` and `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations` to `hard_max_iterations` via `@min(hard_max_iterations, 100)` and `@min(hard_max_iterations, 1000)`. Under the staged Ottawa deck (`hard_max_iterations = 100`), this artificially starves initial STARTE soil chemistry equilibration by 90% (from 1000 down to 100) and inappropriately couples solver convergence bounds to a deck runtime parameter that has no legacy Fortran counterpart.

## Evidence
- `ecosys-ng/src/core/iteration_control.zig:131-132`: applies `@min(hard_max_iterations, 100)` and `@min(hard_max_iterations, 1000)`.
- `f77src/reads.f:126`: legacy reads `NPX,NPY,JOUT,IOUT,KOUT,ICLM` with no runtime record field 6 or hard max iteration ceiling.
- `f77src/solute.f:111`: legacy `PARAMETER (MRXN=60)`.
- `f77src/starte.f:101`: legacy `PARAMETER (MRXN=1000)`.
- `ecosys-ng/src/soil/solute/reaction_solve.zig:1625, 2877`: Zig solute solver iterates `while (iteration < options.max_iterations)` and exits immediately on convergence (`physical_norm <= 1`); on budget exhaustion it returns `error.SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge`.
- `.agent/results/T-00045.md`: SAGE approved decoupling both fields from `hard_max_iterations` under DEV-007 (REJECTED-REVERT).
- `.agent/results/T-00044.md` and `.agent/results/T-00043.md`: established that Zig limits are convergence ceilings while legacy MRXN is a fixed sub-cycle count.

## Proposed mechanism
Removing the `@min(hard_max_iterations, ...)` clamp from `solute_reaction_max_iterations` (setting flat 100) and `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations` (setting flat 1000) in `Limits.fromSceneOptions()` decouples these ceilings from the deck runtime cap. Because Zig's solute solver is an iterative root-finding procedure that terminates immediately upon convergence, the ceilings act purely as fail-loud exhaustion safeguards. Converged chemistry state is invariant to any ceiling greater than or equal to the iterations required for convergence. On budget exhaustion, the solver returns `error.SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge` and rolls back state rather than committing partial iterates.

## Proposed change
1. In `ecosys-ng/src/core/iteration_control.zig`:
   - Decouple `solute_reaction_max_iterations` and `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations` in `Limits.fromSceneOptions()` by removing `@min(hard_max_iterations, ...)`.
   - Update `test "runtime maximum is a hard ceiling for every process budget"` to account for the decoupled fields.
   - Replace `test "staged deck hard_max_iterations clamps SOLUTE and STARTE reaction budgets relative to legacy MRXN"` with `test "SOLUTE and STARTE reaction budgets are pure convergence ceilings decoupled from hard_max_iterations"`. Legacy citation: `f77src/reads.f:126`, `f77src/solute.f:111`, `f77src/starte.f:101`.
2. In `ecosys-ng/src/stages/soil_chemistry_convergence.zig`:
   - Add invariant tests verifying that:
     (a) Converged STARTE initial equilibrium state is bit-identical between ceilings 1000 and 2000.
     (b) Exhaustion of the iteration ceiling returns `error.SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge` and rolls back cell state bit-identically without committing truncated work.

## Prediction
- `Limits.fromSceneOptions(options, 100)` yields `solute_reaction_max_iterations == 100` and `initial_solute_reaction_max_iterations == 1000`.
- The ceilings remain 100 and 1000 regardless of whether `hard_max_iterations` is 7, 60, 100, 200, or 1000.
- Replaying the Ottawa STARTE snapshot at `max_iterations = 1000` and `max_iterations = 2000` yields bit-identical packed cell components.
- Solving with `max_iterations = 1` yields `error.SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge` and leaves the cell state unmutated.

## Falsification condition
- `Limits.fromSceneOptions(options, 100)` produces any value other than 100 and 1000 for the two fields.
- Any bit difference in converged STARTE state between ceiling 1000 and ceiling 2000.
- Exhaustion does not return `error.SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge` or mutates cell state.
- Existing tests fail or regress.
