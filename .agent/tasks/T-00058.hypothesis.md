# HYPOTHESIS: T-00058

## Observed defect
In `ecosys-ng/src/stages/hourly_process_driver.zig`, the dynamic wet precipitation and irrigation input path (`equilibratedDynamicInput`) blends incoming rain and irrigation into a single hypothetical droplet, averages proton activity across the water fraction, and invokes the nonlinear coupled reaction solver (`equilibrateWithWorkspaceAndFailureReport`) with unbuffered floating hydrogen. In the Ottawa run, this non-legacy solver call produces `SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge` at hour 2,589. SAGE (T-00056, T-00057) confirmed that this coupled unbuffered floating-H+ equilibrium has no justification in legacy source and constitutes an unapproved scientific divergence (ledger row DEV-008).

## Evidence
- `f77src/starte.f:127-141`: seeds K=1 rain ions with `CHY1 = 10.0**(-(PHR(NY,NX)-3.0))`.
- `f77src/starte.f:149-163`: seeds K=2 irrigation ions with `CHY1 = 10.0**(-(PHQ(I,NY,NX)-3.0))`.
- `f77src/starte.f:241-242`: closed-form carbonate seed from initial `CHY1`.
- `f77src/starte.f:408, 439`: `DO 1000 M=1,MRXN` runs with `AHY1 = 10.0**(-(PH(L,NY,NX)-3.0))` (where `L=1`) held strictly constant across all 1000 iterations. Hydrogen and carbonate species are never reaction unknowns.
- `f77src/starte.f:780-802`: for `K.NE.3`, all mineral precipitation and surface exchange fluxes are zero; only aqueous solution dissociations relax.
- `f77src/starte.f:1234-1284, 1291-1341`: exports speciated concentrations per source (`*R` for rain, `*Q` for irrigation).
- `f77src/trnsfrs.f:445-528`: linearly mixes each source's speciated concentrations weighted by their separate water fluxes (`X*BLS(1,NY,NX) = FLQGQ(NY,NX)*C*R + FLQGI(NY,NX)*C*Q(I)`), with no re-equilibration.
- `f77src/hour1.f:2463-2466`: updates rain CO2 (`CCOR`) from hourly canopy air temperature Henry's law.
- `ecosys-ng/src/stages/hourly_process_driver.zig:81-142, 603-676`: couples rain and irrigation into a single droplet, mixes pH by water fraction, and runs the full iterative reaction solver.
- `.agent/results/T-00056.md`, `.agent/results/T-00057.md`: SAGE final decision confirming the legacy fixed-pH per-source speciation and linear flux mix.

## Proposed mechanism
Dynamic precipitation and irrigation inputs must follow legacy Fortran semantics:
1. Each active source (rain K=1, irrigation K=2) is speciated independently at its own fixed pH (`CHY1 = 10.0**(-(PH - 3.0))`), using the STARTE M-loop equations with `AHY1` evaluated from topsoil layer pH (`AHY1 = 10.0**(-(soil_ph - 3.0))`).
2. Carbonate species (`CO2`, `HCO3`, `CO3`) and hydrogen remain fixed in the M-loop; all other soluble ion pairs and phosphate species equilibrate.
3. Rain CO2 uses the hourly Henry's-law concentration from air temperature.
4. The two speciated results are combined as a linear flow-weighted flux mix (`flux = water_rain * C_R + water_irrigation * C_Q`), with zero post-mix solve and no water-fraction pH blending.
5. This completely replaces `equilibratedDynamicInput`, eliminating the unbuffered nonlinear solve and preventing `SoluteReactionSolverDidNotConverge`.

## Proposed change
1. In `ecosys-ng/src/stages/hourly_process_driver.zig` (and/or `ecosys-ng/src/soil/chemistry/snow_initialization.zig`), replace `equilibratedDynamicInput` with legacy-equivalent fixed-pH per-source STARTE M-loop speciation followed by the linear flow-weighted mix (`trnsfrs.f:445-528`).
2. Add a matched-state STARTE oracle test validating K=1 rain and K=2 irrigation against legacy STARTE M-loop outputs.

## Prediction
1. Rain (K=1) and irrigation (K=2) inputs under the replaced dynamic input branch will retain their exact source pH (e.g. 7.0 for rain in Ottawa), matching legacy `CHYR = CHY1`.
2. The 41 salt species fluxes and 18 primary species fluxes will equal the exact linear sum of per-source STARTE M-loop speciated concentrations multiplied by their respective water volumes.
3. The matched-state STARTE oracle test will pass, verifying agreement with the legacy `starte.f` equations for K=1 and K=2.
4. The non-converging coupled solver call at the dynamic-input boundary is eliminated.

## Falsification condition
If legacy `starte.f` updates `CHY1` inside `DO 1000 M` for K=1 or K=2, if legacy mixes sources with a post-mix nonlinear solve in `trnsfrs.f`, or if the fixed-pH M-loop speciation produces non-physical or negative concentrations, this hypothesis is falsified and must be rejected.
