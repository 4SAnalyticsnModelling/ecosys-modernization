# HYPOTHESIS: T-00066

## Observed defect
SAGE reviews T-00064 and T-00065 returned VERDICT: REVISE on T-00060 due to two remaining unresolved issues:
1. (R1) In `snow_initialization.zig`, the production 1000-cycle exit check used a loose `attractor_tol = 0.08` (8%) for active cations and anions. That envelope is too wide to distinguish a 20-cycle solve from a 1000-cycle solve (e.g., K=2 Ca differs by 4.5% between M=20 and M=1000) and was falsified by the hypothesis's own <= 1e-11 relative bound.
2. (R3) In `hourly_process_driver.zig`, `var speciation_cache = .{}` was declared locally inside `executeHourlyScience`, resetting every hour. Additionally, `topsoil_hydrogen` was passed as live hourly concentration state rather than evaluating `AHY1 = 10**-(PH(1)-3)` from topsoil activity pH at STARTE events (start, restart, or day change `IDAYR != IOLD` per `soil.f:106-109` and `starte.f:439`), causing hourly changes in topsoil hydrogen to re-speciate.
