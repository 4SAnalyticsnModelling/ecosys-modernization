## FINDING
DECISION: I confirm T-00056. The source-equivalent semantics for dynamic rain and irrigation inputs are as follows. Each source is speciated separately by the STARTE M loop at its own fixed H+ (`CHY1` from `PHR`, or from `PHQ(I)`). Only H+ and the carbonate seeds are fixed; the other species do equilibrate. The two speciated results are then mixed as a linear flow-weighted sum of fluxes, and that mixture is never re-equilibrated. Zig's coupled free-H+ solve on the water-fraction-mixed droplet has no justification in legacy, so under D6 it is REJECT-REPLACE. This is the second SAGE session on this decision, so the decision is now final and governs the FORGE implementation (ledger row DEV-008, `SIGNED-SAGE 2026-09-26 T-00057`).
