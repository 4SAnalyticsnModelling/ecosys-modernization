## FINDING
DECISION: For dynamic precipitation and irrigation inputs, the source-equivalent semantics are fixed-pH legacy speciation, done separately for each source, followed by a linear flow-weighted mix. Zig's coupled free-H+ solve on a single mixed droplet is not source-equivalent. Under D6 it cannot be kept as a deviation, because nothing in the legacy source justifies it, so `equilibratedDynamicInput` must be replaced.

The equivalence model FORGE must implement:
1. Rain (K=1) and irrigation (K=2) are each speciated at their own fixed H+: `CHY1=10**(-(PHR-3))` and `10**(-(PHQ(I)-3))`. H+ is never an unknown.
2. The two speciated concentrations are mixed linearly: `X = FLQGQ*C_R + FLQGI*C_Q(I)`. The mixture is not re-equilibrated, and pH is not blended by water fraction.
3. Rain CO2 follows the hourly `CCOR` Henry's-law override from canopy air temperature.
4. The fixed-pH speciation is recomputed only when STARTE would run: at start-up or when the day changes. Recomputing it every hour at fixed pH is acceptable, provided each hour's inputs equal that day's.

This changes accepted science, so it becomes final only after a second SAGE session confirms it.
