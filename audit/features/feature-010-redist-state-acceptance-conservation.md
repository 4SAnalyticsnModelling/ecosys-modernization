## 5. Net freeze-thaw flux netting and snowpack state acceptance (`redist.f:3935-4218`)

**(a) Net freeze-thaw/evaporation-condensation flux netting for soil layers (`:3935-3955`).** Inside a `DO` loop over layer index (closed by `8575 CONTINUE`), five per-layer sub-hour legs already computed in `watsub.f` (`XWFLFL,XWFLFH,XHFLFL,XWFLVL,XHFLVL` -- freeze-thaw and evaporation-condensation water/heat) are summed into hourly net totals (`TWFLFL,TWFLFH,THFLFL,TWFLVL,THFLVL`). **(b) Snowpack layer geometry, mass, temperature and solute state acceptance (`:3956-4216`).** A `DO 9780 L=1,JS` loop over snowpack layers (JS = max snow layers) that: adds net transport + freeze-thaw + vapor fluxes into `VOLSSL/VOLWSL/VOLVSL/VOLISL` (solid/liquid/vapor/ice, `:3978-3986`); resets fresh-snowfall surface density (`:3999-4011`); computes snow compaction from temperature+overburden (`DDENS1`,`DDENS2`, `:4038-4045`); recomputes layer volume/thickness/cumulative depth (`:4046-4052`); computes layer heat capacity from the `2.095/4.19/1.9274` solid/liquid+vapor/ice specific-heat coefficients and updates temperature from stored enthalpy, gated on a `VHCPWX` heat-capacity floor (`:4069-4088`, with an `ELSE` arm substituting the layer-above's or atmosphere's temperature for a near-empty layer); accumulates column totals `VOLSS/VOLWS/VOLIS/VOLS/DPTHS` (`:4089-4093`); resets an inactive layer's storage to zero and inherits temperature/density from the layer above (`:4116-4132`); and finally accumulates snowpack solute/salt content (`:4143-4215`, `ISALTG`-gated for salts).
