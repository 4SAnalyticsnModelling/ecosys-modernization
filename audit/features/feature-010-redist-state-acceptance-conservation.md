## 5. Net freeze-thaw flux netting and snowpack state acceptance (`redist.f:3935-4218`)

Read in full. Two structurally distinct sub-blocks:

**(a) Net freeze-thaw/evaporation-condensation flux netting for soil layers (`:3935-3955`).** Inside a `DO` loop over layer index (closed by `8575 CONTINUE`), five per-layer sub-hour legs already computed in `watsub.f` (`XWFLFL,XWFLFH,XHFLFL,XWFLVL,XHFLVL` -- freeze-thaw and evaporation-condensation water/heat) are summed into hourly net totals (`TWFLFL,TWFLFH,THFLFL,TWFLVL,THFLVL`). This is pure pass-through bookkeeping, not a phase-change computation -- these net totals are exactly the terms consumed by Finding 1's `VOLW=VOLW+TFLW+TWFLVL+TWFLFL+...` state-acceptance statement, i.e. this sub-block is further upstream of Finding 1, not a new mechanism.

**(b) Snowpack layer geometry, mass, temperature and solute state acceptance (`:3956-4216`).** A `DO 9780 L=1,JS` loop over snowpack layers (JS = max snow layers) that: adds net transport + freeze-thaw + vapor fluxes into `VOLSSL/VOLWSL/VOLVSL/VOLISL` (solid/liquid/vapor/ice, `:3978-3986`); resets fresh-snowfall surface density (`:3999-4011`); computes snow compaction from temperature+overburden (`DDENS1`,`DDENS2`, `:4038-4045`); recomputes layer volume/thickness/cumulative depth (`:4046-4052`); computes layer heat capacity from the `2.095/4.19/1.9274` solid/liquid+vapor/ice specific-heat coefficients and updates temperature from stored enthalpy, gated on a `VHCPWX` heat-capacity floor (`:4069-4088`, with an `ELSE` arm substituting the layer-above's or atmosphere's temperature for a near-empty layer); accumulates column totals `VOLSS/VOLWS/VOLIS/VOLS/DPTHS` (`:4089-4093`); resets an inactive layer's storage to zero and inherits temperature/density from the layer above (`:4116-4132`); and finally accumulates snowpack solute/salt content (`:4143-4215`, `ISALTG`-gated for salts).

## 7. Fire/combustion state acceptance (`redist.f:10680-10973`)

Read in full. Confirmed, per the assignment's caution, that this is a **different** mechanism from `grosub.f`'s plant-canopy fire cascade (`issue-026`): this range is soil-solid-fuel combustion *product acceptance* -- it takes combustion rates already computed in `nitro.f` (`RCGSK`, `RCBOMC/N/P`, `RCBORC/N/P`, `RCBOQC/A/N/P`, `RCBOHC/A/N/P`, `RCBOSC/N/P`) and in `trnsfr.f` (`RCGOX`, `RCHOX`, `RC4OX`) and distributes the products into charcoal, gas emissions and mineralization. It splits into two physically distinct sub-parts:

**(a) `:10684-10932` soil combustion product partition, gated `IF(ICHKF.EQ.1)`.** For every layer `L=0,NL`, computes an O2-limited fraction `FCC=(RCGSK-RCGOX-RCHOX)/RCGSK` (charcoal share) and `FCBOX/FCBCH` (aerobic-CO2 vs anaerobic-CH4 split of the non-charcoal share, both already O2/CH4-limited upstream in `trnsfr.f`), plus temperature-dependent N/P mineralization-vs-gas fractions `FCOMN=FCOMNY+(FCOMNX-FCOMNY)*(1-TFNCOS)` and `FCOMP` (using the `:196` `PARAMETER` constants from Finding 6a). Every combustion-rate family (microbial biomass, microbial residue, DOC/DOA/DON/DOP, adsorbed OM, SOM, charcoal) is run through the same seven-way split: charcoal to litter (`OSC(5,...)`), CO2 (`COX`), CH4 (`CHX`), NH4 mineralization (`ZNH4S`), gaseous N loss (`ZOX`), H2PO4 mineralization (`H2PO4`), gaseous P loss (`POX`). Heat release `HCBFL` uses `GCBCO/GCBCH/GCBC4` combustion-energy constants from `starts.f`. Layer totals accumulate into landscape diagnostics `TLRSDC/TLNH4/TLPO4/ZN2GIN/TPIN` and fire-specific ledgers `VCOXFS/VCO2G/VCH4G/VNOXG/VPOXG` (`:10919-10928`).

**(b) `:10934-10971` ground-surface secondary CH4 combustion.** A separate, genuinely different computation (not just acceptance of an upstream rate): gated on `TKQGX>TCMBX` (ground-surface air temperature exceeds the 473.15 K combustion threshold from `starts.f`), it computes `RC4OK` via Michaelis-Menten kinetics on the *canopy-air* CH4/O2 mixing ratios (`CH4Q`, `OXYQ`, half-saturation constants `CCH4GK=10`, `COXYGK=2100` umol/mol from `starts.f`), capped by both the already-present net CH4 flux `-XHNET` and the remaining canopy O2 budget `(OXYC-XONET)/2.667`, then adds the combusted amount back into `XONET/XCNET/XHNET` and the canopy heat ledger `HCBFG`. This is structurally the same formula as `extract.f:175-181`'s per-PFT canopy CH4 combustion (`ROGOK`/`RC4OK`, gated on `TKC`/`TKD` instead of `TKQGX`) -- an "N parallel blocks" pair (per-PFT canopy air vs. bulk ground-surface canopy air), both driven by the same `TCMBX/CCH4GK/COXYGK` constants from `starts.f`.

Zig homes for sub-part (a), confirmed live and production-wired (not dead code -- verified by finding actual callers in `ecosys_ng.zig:5127,5140,5220,5285`, all gated on `driver_context.fire_active_this_hour.*[cell]`, the direct analogue of `ICHKF`): combustion-fraction computation and pool depletion in `ecosys-ng/src/soil/organic/combustion.zig` (sha256 `49789617EDE478B65E2FA39A12883D1383B694CF39C1A1816B1A8614BBB25548`) -- `regularCombustionFraction`/`charcoalCombustionFraction`/`burnOrganicStateLayer`/`burnSurfaceOrganicStateCell` (`:101-258`), citing `nitro.f` line ranges (this module is the *rate computation* side, i.e. `nitro.f`'s own combustion-rate derivation, reused rather than re-split); the product partition itself (charcoal/CO2/CH4/NH4/gas-N/H2PO4/gas-P split matching Finding 7a's seven-way split) lives in `ecosys-ng/src/soil/biogeochemistry/organic_matter_fire_exchange.zig` (sha256 `7D707EDBECAC35DCBFACB3CC2DA37589FB588CE436AFE78DE6086BE58F1419E5`): `finalizeLayer`/`finalizeSurfaceCell` (`:363-567`) reproduce the `FCOMN`/`FCOMP` temperature-dependent fraction formula exactly -- `ammonium_fraction = 0.1 + (0.5-0.1)*(1-response)` and `phosphate_fraction = 0.7 + (0.9-0.7)*(1-response)` at `:386-387` are `FCOMNY+(FCOMNX-FCOMNY)*(1-TFNCOS)` and `FCOMPY+(FCOMPX-FCOMPY)*(1-TFNCOS)` with the exact `0.1/0.5/0.7/0.9` constants from `redist.f:196`.
