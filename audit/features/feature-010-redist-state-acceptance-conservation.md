## 4. Runoff and subsurface domain-edge boundary fluxes (`redist.f:616-1439`)

Read in full. Header "RUNOFF AND SUBSURFACE BOUNDARY FLUXES" -- an outer `DO 9985 L=NU..NL` (soil layer) around a `DO 9980 N=1,3` / `DO 9975 NN=1,2` (direction/side) pair that locates the six grid-cell faces (east/west via `N=1`, south/north via `N=2`, bottom-only via `N=3`, `NN=2` skipped -- `:628-683`) and derives destination indices `N4,N5,N6` and a sign `XN` (`+1`=gain,`-1`=loss). This is the same "identify true external boundary face" pattern as the internal relayering loop already documented under Finding 2, but applied to domain-edge export/import instead of intra-column layer transfer.

For `N.NE.3` at the top active layer only (`:694`): runoff export of water/heat (`QR,HQR`→`CRUN,URUN,HEATOU`, `:694-704`), dissolved C/N/P (`:726-756`→`TCOU,TZOU,TPOU,UDOCQ/UDICQ/UDONQ/UDINQ/UDOPQ/UDIPQ`), O2/H2 (`:748-751`→`OXYGOU,H2GOU`), `ISALTG`-gated salts (`:785-812`→`TPOU,TIONOU,UIONOU`), a diagnostic-only electrical-conductivity term (`ECNDQ`, `:824-845`, never accumulated into state), snow-drift water/heat/C/N/P/salts (`:849-943`, same ledgers), and `IERSNG`-gated sediment-erosion export (adsorbed/precipitated N/P, microbial-residue and DOC/humus C, sediment-borne salts; `:960-1132`→`TSEDOU,USEDOU` plus the same C/N/P/ion ledgers). For every layer (`NCN.NE.3.OR.N.EQ.3`, `:1152`): subsurface water/heat flux (`FLW,FLWH,HFLW`→`VOLWOU,HVOLO,UVOLO,HEATOU`; artificial-drainage subset `FLWY,FLWHY`→`UVOLY`, `:1152-1166`), dissolved+gas-phase C/N/P (`:1191-1236`), O2/H2 (`:1250-1259`), `ISALTG`-gated solutes (`:1294-1371`), and a second diagnostic-only conductivity term (`ECNDX`, `:1393-1430`).

Zig: `ecosys-ng/src/validation/landscape_boundary_balance.zig` (sha256 `35E783020BF029982042F99A2751C1B9B6693BE835BF0D10365078CE43B61E1C`) is the confirmed production home -- it is re-exported as `landscape_boundary_ledger` (`ecosys-ng/src/module_index.zig:34`) and called from the hourly stage driver (e.g. `stages/hourly_sediment.zig:322` for sediment C/N/P/ion export; confirmed via `Grep`, not just the module's own claim). ## 5. Net freeze-thaw flux netting and snowpack state acceptance (`redist.f:3935-4218`)

Read in full. Two structurally distinct sub-blocks:

**(a) Net freeze-thaw/evaporation-condensation flux netting for soil layers (`:3935-3955`).** Inside a `DO` loop over layer index (closed by `8575 CONTINUE`), five per-layer sub-hour legs already computed in `watsub.f` (`XWFLFL,XWFLFH,XHFLFL,XWFLVL,XHFLVL` -- freeze-thaw and evaporation-condensation water/heat) are summed into hourly net totals (`TWFLFL,TWFLFH,THFLFL,TWFLVL,THFLVL`). This is pure pass-through bookkeeping, not a phase-change computation -- these net totals are exactly the terms consumed by Finding 1's `VOLW=VOLW+TFLW+TWFLVL+TWFLFL+...` state-acceptance statement, i.e. this sub-block is further upstream of Finding 1, not a new mechanism.

**(b) Snowpack layer geometry, mass, temperature and solute state acceptance (`:3956-4216`).** A `DO 9780 L=1,JS` loop over snowpack layers (JS = max snow layers) that: adds net transport + freeze-thaw + vapor fluxes into `VOLSSL/VOLWSL/VOLVSL/VOLISL` (solid/liquid/vapor/ice, `:3978-3986`); resets fresh-snowfall surface density (`:3999-4011`); computes snow compaction from temperature+overburden (`DDENS1`,`DDENS2`, `:4038-4045`); recomputes layer volume/thickness/cumulative depth (`:4046-4052`); computes layer heat capacity from the `2.095/4.19/1.9274` solid/liquid+vapor/ice specific-heat coefficients and updates temperature from stored enthalpy, gated on a `VHCPWX` heat-capacity floor (`:4069-4088`, with an `ELSE` arm substituting the layer-above's or atmosphere's temperature for a near-empty layer); accumulates column totals `VOLSS/VOLWS/VOLIS/VOLS/DPTHS` (`:4089-4093`); resets an inactive layer's storage to zero and inherits temperature/density from the layer above (`:4116-4132`); and finally accumulates snowpack solute/salt content (`:4143-4215`, `ISALTG`-gated for salts).

Zig homes -- all in `ecosys-ng/src/soil/water/`, each independently marked **"A8a DISPOSITION: superseded"** in its own banner (same disposition class as Finding 4's sediment module, tagged to a `docs/traceability/a1b_snowpack_minimum_heat_capacity_unbound.md` path that, like the erosion doc, does not exist in this checkout -- confirmed by `Glob`):
- `snowpack_phase_inventory_update.zig` (sha256 `7E77BEDA2FA9981A8B9A1C3DF2C006728606B987CC26C283F0A630958D24E537`) reconstructs sub-block (a)'s snow-specific analogue (`redist.f:3960-3986`) but its own header explains why it is unbound: production distributes the four phase legs across dedicated owners by *mechanism* rather than applying pre-computed `XWFLF*`-style increments -- freeze-thaw mass/heat is `snow_phase_change.zig` (sha256 `3F95087F6D903569F01030C65F7CF8FAB7C6603C8111CDCDA92B8DD678FD68EF`, Newton step with Picard fallback, clamped to available latent heat, then mass moved), vapor exchange is `snow_vapor_equilibrium.zig`, and net transport is split across `snow_solute_transport.zig`/`snow_relayering.zig`. Column totals are deliberately not stored anywhere (accumulated at the point of consumption instead, e.g. `stages/hourly_science_snow_energy.zig:124-128`) to avoid a second copy going stale.
- `snowpack_geometry_temperature_update.zig` (sha256 `CAF509B357B3F0D0FF615DB92E8F52376C50D8668051D641B995D49A49A7B3FD`) reconstructs sub-block (b)'s geometry/heat-capacity/column-total/inactive-reset logic (`redist.f:4046-4133`) but is likewise unbound: geometry is owned by `snow_solute_transport.zig:173-186`, heat capacity is recomputed at five separate production sites (`snow_relayering.zig`, `snow_vapor_equilibrium.zig`, `snow_solute_transport.zig`), and -- most importantly -- **the legacy temperature-update formula (`redist.f:4077-4087`, `TKW=(ENGYW+THFLWW+XHFLF0+XHFLV0)/VHCPW`) is *intentionally* not reproduced anywhere**, because by the time this point in the hourly order is reached, `snow_heat_conduction.zig:104-111` and `snow_phase_change.zig:73-90` have already written `temperature_k` via their own implicit/Newton solves; reapplying the legacy increment would double-count. Confirmed independently (not just the module's own claim) by reading the cited call order.
- `snow_compaction.zig` (sha256 `A6F9911D514B6BF952FC3071051C65771D57BC8E89048989EEBF1FFD9B84F125`) owns the `:4116-4132` inactive-layer reset with one noted, accepted divergence: `redist.f:4127-4130` inherits `DENSS` from `L-1` for an inactive layer, Zig leaves the previously-stored density untouched instead -- the two agree whenever the layer above was itself inactive and the value is provably unread while `solid==0`, so this is recorded as a non-divergence, not filed as a gap.

Neither sub-block computes the Dall'Amico freezing-point-depression formula itself (that is `watsub.f`/`phase_change.zig`, already the subject of `issue-024`) -- both are downstream **acceptance/bookkeeping** of already-computed fluxes, and (b) is specifically about above-ground *snowpack* layers, a physically distinct state from the *soil* layer-1 (0.01 m topsoil) state that `issue-024` concerns. **No concretely relevant new evidence for issue-024's two open candidates (matric/osmotic-potential value; substep-schedule gap) was found in this range** -- the snowpack's freeze-thaw treatment (`snow_phase_change.zig`: enthalpy/latent-heat-limited Newton-Picard) is a different, non-competing mechanism from the soil's Dall'Amico matric-potential-based method, and the soil-layer net-flux netting in sub-block (a) is pure summation with no bearing on either candidate. Per the assignment's instruction, issue-024 itself is therefore **not touched** -- nothing is added there.

**Disposition: `preserved`** for the underlying physics/bookkeeping (same terms, same order, same coefficients, confirmed at five separate production sites); **`replaced-by-approved-feature`** for the acceptance architecture (distributed-by-mechanism ownership with atomic per-owner validation, vs. the Fortran's single monolithic sequential block) -- consistent with the "superseded" disposition class already established for Finding 4's sediment module, not a new pattern.
