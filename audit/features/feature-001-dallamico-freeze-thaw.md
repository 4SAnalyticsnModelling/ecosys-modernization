Implements Dall'Amico et al. Consumes a `mualem_van_genuchten` parameter set (validated via `.validate()`) -- i.e. this Dall'Amico implementation is coupled to the *new* Mualem-van Genuchten retention curve (see `feature-002-mualem-van-genuchten.md`), not the legacy log-log curve, for its unfrozen retention inverse.

## Scientific and numerical tests
Expected effects and unaffected quantities: not yet assessed.

Parameter/domain/edge tests: the in-source comment at `phase_change.zig` (tag `DALLAMICO-BELOW-RESIDUAL-WATER-HOUR-2726-001`) documents a specific, real, dated edge-case investigation already performed by whoever wrote this code: two observed model states (hour 2,705: content=0, residual=9.5e-2, T=312.12 K; hour 2,726: content=3.4687218985266395e-2, residual=9.5e-2, T=319.80 K) where a surface layer is baked dry by evaporation to below the van-Genuchten residual water content, above the melting point. The note explains why this is a *legitimate physical state*, not a defect: the function's retention inverse is evaluated at a pressure head (not a content), and `liquid_water_m3 = min(total_water_equivalent_m3, retention_limited)` already caps liquid at what the layer can hold, making `ice = total - liquid` non-negative by construction regardless of the residual bound -- residual water content bounds *capillary* processes, not evaporative removal. The note also records a **previously attempted and reverted fix**: an unconditional `ice = 0` short-circuit for this case, which "produced `SoilHeatSolverStagnated` at the same hour" because it created a genuine discontinuity (correct at 46.7 C, wrong below freezing) that broke the heat solver's Newton iteration. This is exactly the kind of before/after, falsifiable, dated experiment record the audit contract asks for -- it was just written directly in source rather than in `audit/issues/`. Recommend copying/linking this into the formal issue register rather than re-deriving it (see handoff note on embedded `TAG-NNN` provenance comments).
