### 4. Fire-gas-release sections (litter `trnsfr.f:1080-1146`, soil `trnsfr.f:1265-1329`) -- `preserved`

Read in full, including the setup lines that feed them (`:703-723`, scaling `RCGSK`/`ROGSK` from `nitro.f`'s already-computed total combustion carbon by the gas sub-timestep `XNPG`). Both blocks are structurally identical (litter layer `0`, soil layer `L`): given `RCGSK2`/`ROGSK2` (total combustion carbon and its stoichiometric O2 demand, `2.667=32/12`), each partitions the combustion product into an O2-limited aerobic CO2 fraction (`ROGOX2`/`RCGOX2`, Michaelis-Menten on ambient O2 concentration vs `COXYGL`, scaled by a charcoal-vs-gas split `FCOGC=1-FCOMCX*(1-TFNCOS)`), a residual CH4 production term (`RCHOX2`, the non-aerobic remainder scaled by the analogous `FCHGC` charcoal split), and a further CH4-oxidation term limited by both remaining O2 and CH4 concentration (`RC4OX2`). This is a **third, distinct fire/combustion-adjacent mechanism** from the two already audited this session: it is neither the biomass-combustion-carbon computation itself (`grosub.f`/`feature-020`, `shoot_fire.zig`'s `canopyFireCombustion`, which computes *how much* carbon a fire consumes) nor the ground-surface/canopy-air secondary-CH4-combustion bookkeeping (`redist.f:10934-10971`/`issue-031`, which acts on the bulk canopy-air CH4/O2 mixing ratio after the hour's net flux has already accumulated). This `trnsfr.f` mechanism sits between them: given an already-known total combustion-carbon flux, it determines the O2-limited *gas-phase* split between CO2, CH4, and charcoal for the litter and soil pore-air spaces specifically. No species-completeness gap: only C/O2 participate in combustion oxidation chemistry here, by physical necessity; N/P combustion products are partitioned separately (confirmed downstream, not in this exact block).

Zig: `ecosys-ng/src/soil/biogeochemistry/organic_matter_fire_exchange.zig` (`State.finalizeLayer`/`State.finalizeSurfaceCell`, doc-cited `trnsfr.f 1111/1292` for `ROGOX`/`oxygen_limited_uptake_g` and `trnsfr.f 1114-1116/1295-1297` for `RC4OX`/`methane_combustion_g_carbon`) and `ecosys-ng/src/plant/exchange/soil.zig`'s `subsurfaceOrganicMatterFire` (doc-commented "TRNSFR/REDIST gas partition shared by litter, soil organic matter, and roots"). Verified the algebra term-for-term: `unlimited_oxygen_g=total_combusted_carbon_g*2.667` maps to `ROGSK2`; `aerobic_oxygen_g=min(unlimited_oxygen_g*O2frac,oxygen_content)*aerobic_gas_fraction` maps to `ROGOX2` exactly, including the charcoal-split multiplier (`aerobic_gas_fraction`==legacy `FCOGC`, `parameters.maximum_aerobic_charcoal_fraction`==legacy `FCOMCX`, `combustion_temperature_response`==legacy `TFNCOS`); `methane_produced_g_carbon` maps to `RCHOX2`; `methane_oxidized_g_carbon=min(...,methane_content,oxygen_remaining/2.667)` maps to `RC4OX2` exactly, with an added `@max(0,...)` defensive floor not present in the legacy AMIN1 chain (same style of added safety clamp as this dossier's finding #1). Both litter (`finalizeSurfaceCell`) and soil (`finalizeLayer`) call the one shared `subsurfaceOrganicMatterFire` kernel, correctly mirroring the legacy structural duplication. Well tested: `organic_matter_fire_exchange.zig`'s "combined soil root fire finalization atomically conserves products" test explicitly checks `oxygen_consumption_g_o[0] == oxygen_limited_uptake_g_o[0] + methane_combustion_g_c[0]*2.667` (the exact `ROGOX2+RC4OX2*2.667` legacy identity) and bounds `RC4OX`-equivalent by both source `AMIN1` arms independently.

**Disposition: `preserved`.** High confidence; exact term-for-term formula correspondence, explicit line citations on the Zig side, existing regression coverage. No issue filed.

### 5. Litter/atmosphere and soil-surface/atmosphere gas boundary-layer exchange (`trnsfr.f:3194-3916`) -- `preserved`

Read in full (actual extent `:3194-3916`, ending at the `SOLUTE FLUXES BETWEEN ADJACENT GRID CELLS` banner `:3918` -- the assignment's `:3194-3686` was a prior pass's approximate pointer, not the true block boundary). Two structurally parallel blocks, litter layer `0` (`:3200-3523`) and soil surface layer `NU` (`:3532-3916`), each implementing three physical mechanisms for all 7 non-salt gases (CO2, CH4, O2, N2, N2O, NH3, H2 -- confirmed complete, no 8th/omitted species, no "N-1 of N" gap):

1. **Diffusive boundary flux**: effective in-medium diffusivity (`DFLG2`, Penman-tortuosity form) in series with the atmospheric boundary-layer conductance (`PARR*`/`PARG*`, each gas's own fixed multiplier of the generic `PARRM`/`PARGM`: 0.74/1.04/0.83/0.86/0.74/1.02/2.08 for CO2/CH4/O2/N2/N2O/NH3/H2 respectively).
2. **Convective (pressure/temperature-driven) bulk flux** (`VGFLW`, from the ideal-gas molar-content mismatch `VTATM-VTGAS` at litter/soil temperature, constant `1.2194E+04`), allocated across species in proportion to each gas's molar content.
3. **Air-water phase exchange/volatilization-dissolution** (`R*DFG`, `DFGS`-rate-constant equilibration between gaseous and solubility-weighted aqueous content).

Zig: all three mechanisms are implemented as shared, generic functions in `ecosys-ng/src/soil/gas/transport.zig` and wired to both litter and soil-surface boundaries identically through `ecosys-ng/src/soil/gas/atmosphere_exchange.zig`'s `Boundary`/`calculateFluxesG`(`FromValidatedInputs`):
- Diffusive: `atmosphericDiffusiveFluxG`/`FromValidatedInputs` (`transport.zig:452-479`), doc-commented "Exact bounded TRNSFRS[*] atmospheric diffusion convention." The per-gas boundary multiplier array `atmospheric_boundary_multiplier = {0.74, 1.04, 0.83, 0.86, 0.74, 1.02, 2.08}` (`transport.zig:23`) matches `PARRCO`/`PARRCH`/`PARROX`/`PARRNG`/`PARRN2`/`PARRN3`/`PARRH2` exactly, species-for-species, confirmed by the existing test `"species multipliers reproduce atmospheric boundary conductances"`.
- Convective: `pressureDrivenFluxesG`/`FromValidatedInputs` (`transport.zig:504-531`), doc-commented "Pressure/temperature correction from TRNSFRS[*]," reusing the exact legacy ideal-gas constant `1.2194e4` and reproducing `VGFLW`'s molar-proportional species allocation.
- Phase exchange: `phaseExchangeFluxG`/`FromValidatedInputs` (`transport.zig:569-584`) reproduces `R*DFG`'s `DFGS*(gas*equivalent_water_volume - dissolved*air_volume)/total_equivalent_volume` form exactly, with an added `clamp(-dissolved_mass,gaseous_mass)` defensive bound not present in the legacy formula.

[*] Minor documentation-accuracy nit, not a scientific issue: both `pressureDrivenFluxesG`'s and `bubblingFluxesGFromValidatedInputs`'s doc comments say "from TRNSFRS," but `trnsfrs.f` (salts) has no gas phase and confirmed **zero** matches for `VTATM`/`VGFLW`/`1.2194E+04` in `f77src/trnsfrs.f`; the actual source is `trnsfr.f` (no trailing S). Cosmetic only -- the adjacent code-level citations elsewhere in the same functions correctly say `trnsfr.f:5303-5306,5777-5778`. Not worth its own issue file; noting here so a future reader isn't misled into searching the wrong source file.

**Cross-reference strengthening existing finding #3 / `issue-011`**: the legacy code at this line range shows the same litter-vs-soil-surface asymmetry pattern as `issue-011`'s already-closed finding, one line-range earlier than previously cited. Litter's diffusive flux (`:3282-3295`) is `AMIN1`/`AMAX1`-bounded toward the atmospheric equilibrium endpoint in both directions; the soil-surface diffusive flux at the structurally parallel location (`:3612-3618`) is an **unbounded** direct assignment (`DFVCOG=DCO2GQ*(CCO2E(NY,NX)-CCO2G2)`, no `AMIN1` at all). This is the same disposition already recorded for `issue-011`/finding #3 (a `legacy-defect-corrected`-flavored uniform safety bound, already fixed and tested) -- recorded here as corroborating evidence extending that finding's citation range to `:3268-3295,3612-3618`, not a new issue.

## Addendum 2026-09-18 (same session, third pass): vertical macropore netting asymmetry -- closed out with a located Zig counterpart

Direct re-read of `trnsfr.f:4373-4522` confirms the asymmetry described above: the `FLWHM.GT.0.0` branch nets each donor concentration against the donor cell's own lateral macro-micro exchange flux under `N.EQ.3.AND.VOLAH(N6,N5,N4).GT.VOLWHM(...)` (`:4396`), while the mirrored `FLWHM.LT.0.0` branch (`:4491-4521`) has no analog of that check or netting at all -- the fifth confirmed instance of this project's recurring directional-asymmetry defect pattern (after issue-017 x2, issue-018, issue-020, issue-021).

The Zig counterpart is now located: `driver/transport_step.zig` wires vertical macropore transport through a symmetric, iterative, conductance-based face solve (`soil/gas/aqueous_extensive_transport.zig`'s `advance`/`solve`) with the lateral macro-micro exchange applied as a separate, subsequent, direction-agnostic step (`soil/solute/transport.zig`'s `calculateConvectivePoreExchangeFlux`/`poreExchange`). This architecture has no equivalent of `trnsfr.f:4396`'s ad hoc netting term in *either* direction -- Zig does not inherit the asymmetry, but only because the whole forward-difference netting mechanism was replaced by a bounded, transactional iterative solve, not because the asymmetry was identified and symmetrically fixed. Whether that iterative solve's own conservation acceptance already provides an equivalent safeguard against the double-depletion scenario the legacy netting term defended against was not proven with a targeted edge-case test this pass. **Disposition: `unresolved`**, filed as `audit/issues/issue-029-trnsfr-vertical-macropore-netting-directional-asymmetry.md`.
