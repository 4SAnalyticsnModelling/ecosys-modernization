## Failure signature and first bad time/location/process

Not a run-time failure signature -- this is a source-level architecture finding surfaced while auditing the legacy total-ion-activity/electrical-conductivity computation `CION`/`CSTR`/`A1`/`A2`/`A3`/`ECND` (`f77src/hour1.f:4001-4078`), which is the sole input to the osmotic water-potential term `PSISO=-8.3143E-06*TKS*CION` (`hour1.f:4181`, and again for litter at `hour1.f:4488`). `PSISO` feeds `PSIST` (total water potential, `hour1.f:4183`), which in turn is one of the two live, unresolved candidates in `issue-024` ("a different matric-plus-osmotic potential value feeding the [freeze-thaw] formula").

On the Zig side, there are **two independent implementations of the identical extended Debye-Huckel formula**, both textually verified against `hour1.f:4013-4078`/`4432-4447`:

1. `ecosys-ng/src/soil/chemistry/ionic_strength_conductivity.zig` -- a byte-for-byte, source-order translation of `hour1.f:4013-4078`, operating on the exact fixed named-species arrays the legacy code lists (`ZAL`, `ZFE`, `ZCA`, `ZMG`, ... one slice per legacy variable, dimensions asserted in `validateDimensions`). Its own banner states: **"A5 DISPOSITION: never production bound... architecturally superseded... Do not bind this module, and do not delete it: the tests are a source-order comparison oracle."**
2. `ecosys-ng/src/soil/solute/activity_coefficients.zig` (`calculate`) + `ecosys-ng/src/soil/solute/charge_classification.zig` (`classify`) -- the actual production-bound path, reached via `ecosys-ng/src/soil/solute/chemistry_state.zig:708` (`State.activityCoefficients`) and consumed for the osmotic term at `ecosys-ng/src/soil/runtime/hourly_workspace.zig:159-161`. This computes the *same* extended Debye-Huckel law (identical `0.509`/`9`/`4`/`0.20` constants, identical ionic-strength/activity-coefficient/total-activity/conductivity forms -- confirmed by direct read of both files) but sources its charge totals from the full dynamic solute-reaction-network species state (`chemistry_state.State.aqueous`/`non_band_phosphate`/`band_phosphate`), not from a fixed named-array snapshot.

Module 1's own banner asserts module 2 is the deliberate, documented replacement ("Superseded by: `solute_activity_coefficients`/`solute_ion_activities`... The legacy form is a three-charge Debye-Huckel shortcut over a fixed species list. The solute activity network resolves activities over the full runtime species set."). That claim is plausible and the *formula* equivalence is confirmed by direct comparison of the two `.zig` files (done this pass). **What has not been checked by any pass to date is whether the two pipelines' *inputs* (not just their formulas) agree** for a specific cell/hour -- i.e., whether the full dynamic species-network state that feeds module 2, when summed into the same three charge-classes, numerically reproduces what the legacy soil-file-derived, largely-static (`ISALTG=0` for a deck like Ottawa, per `hour1.f`'s own comment: "salt concentrations entered in soil file generate equilibrium concentrations that remain static during model run") species values would give if run through module 1's arrays.

This matters specifically for `issue-024`: that issue's round 3/4 resolution lists, as one of its two remaining live candidates, "a different matric-plus-osmotic potential value feeding the [freezing-point] formula" -- and `PSISO` (the osmotic half of that potential) is entirely determined by whichever `CION` pipeline is live. If the dynamic solute network's initial species pools do not exactly match what the static soil-file values would produce (e.g., different equilibration state at t=0/hour 1, different completeness of the tracked species list, or a different reference volume/units convention), `PSISO` -- and therefore the freezing-point threshold `TFREEZ`/`freezing_temperature` that both issue-024's candidate and this issue share a dependency on -- could diverge specifically at hour 1, specifically for the layer(s) issue-024 already flagged.

## Scientific/output impact

If real, this would affect the near-surface soil osmotic potential -> total water potential -> freezing-point-depression threshold chain for every layer, every hour, throughout any run -- not just hour 1. It is flagged here specifically because of its direct relevance to `issue-024`'s still-open top-soil-layer water/freeze-thaw divergence (round 3/4 candidate list), but it is a distinct, independently-filed mechanism from that issue's other live candidate (the substep-schedule/`ICHKV`-equivalent-tier gap) and from the already-ruled-out issue-048 litter-heat-capacity candidate.
