### Step 3: is it a layer-index/relayering misattribution (hypothesis c)? Ruled out by an existing internal self-check

### Refined hypothesis (not confirmed by execution this pass): the shared carrier is `dry_reference_water_m3[layer]`, and the defect is in its captured VALUE, not the guard's threshold
