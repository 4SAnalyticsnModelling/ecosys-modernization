## Reframe decision

**Working conclusion (not proven, but the best-supported explanation available)**: this is very likely the same fundamental stiff-convergence numerical wall the mature reference project's own maintainers already characterized and could not resolve through translation-defect-hunting, encountered somewhat earlier in this checkout (hour 2,578 vs. their 2,604-2,704) for a reason neither this session nor the peer session could pin down with a clean third experiment.
