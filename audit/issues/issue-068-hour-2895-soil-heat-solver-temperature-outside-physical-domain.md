- **Physically, the coupling is real and matters for what the attempt actually buys.** Reading `residualAt` (`phase_solver.zig`, the per-cell loop) line by line: `vaporLiquidEquilibrium(scratch[temperature], ...)` and `matrixFreezeThaw(scratch[temperature], ...)` both take the TRIAL temperature as a direct input, and their outputs (`vapor_change`, `matrix_freeze`, `macro_freeze`) are what `target[matrix_water]`/`target[vapor]`/`target[matrix_ice]`/`target[macro_water]`/`target[macro_ice]` are built from via `applyChange`. The mass-side residual is `target[...] - trial[...]`. So even when the trial's mass *inputs* are held fixed at their full-step values, the mass *targets* -- and therefore the mass residuals -- are recomputed as a function of whatever temperature is tried. **The premise that leaving the mass side "untouched" preserves its already-good (machine-noise-scale) residual is not correct by construction; it is only true if the resulting hybrid point happens to still be close to a fixed point, which is exactly what the unmodified `scaledNorm<=1` check re-verifies rather than assumes.** This does not make the attempt invalid or not-worth-trying (the task's own step 1 asked whether it is "mathematically sensible," not whether it is guaranteed to help) -- it is a legitimate, honestly-checked box-constrained Newton backtracking variant with a per-coordinate (diagonal) step-length matrix instead of a single scalar, a standard technique. But it does mean the intuition motivating this variant ("the mass side is already good, so don't disturb it") is only partially true: the mass *inputs* are preserved, but the mass *residuals* are not held fixed as a side effect, because the governing phase-equilibrium equations couple mass targets to trial temperature.

**Conclusion of step 1: separable enough to implement (mechanically legal, and the honest acceptance re-check means it can never fabricate success), but the coupling means it is not guaranteed to outperform uniform damping, and should be tried in addition to, not instead of, the ninth round's kept mechanism.**
