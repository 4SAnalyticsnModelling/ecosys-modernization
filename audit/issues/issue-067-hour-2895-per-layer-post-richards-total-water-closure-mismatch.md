## Investigation (2026-09-20, read-only/static-analysis pass, no build/run)

Read the check's raise site in full: `ecosys-ng/src/soil/water/heat_step.zig:3437-3506` (`validatePerLayerPostRichardsTotalWaterClosure`) and its production call site (`:2098-2109`), plus everything between the two `post_richards_total_water_equivalent_m3`/`post_phase_external_water_change_m3` snapshot points (`:1680-1980`) and the `postPhasePreHeat` hook that actually publishes `boundary_gain_m3` (`ecosys-ng/src/stages/hourly_heat_water_solute.zig:6632-6790`) and the ground-vapor-exchange primitive it calls (`ecosys-ng/src/surface/ground_vapor_exchange.zig:1-141`).

**What the check does.** For each active soil layer, per hour: `before_m3` is the layer's total water-equivalent (matrix+macropore liquid, matrix+macropore ice, vapor) captured immediately after the Richards solve; `after_m3` is the same sum re-read at the very end of the hourly water/heat transaction (after vapor transport, phase change, the post-phase ground-vapor-exchange hook, and the spatial heat solve). The check requires `after_m3 == before_m3 + vapor_internal_gain_m3[layer] - phase_displacement_m3[layer] + boundary_gain_m3[layer]` within `scoped_conservation.evaluate`'s absolute-plus-relative tolerance (`water_conservation_absolute_tolerance_m * cell_area` + `water_conservation_relative_tolerance * scale`, plus a `representation_floor_m3 = 4096 * eps(f64) * activity_scale_m3` roundoff allowance, where `activity_scale_m3 = max(|before_m3|, |after_m3|, |expected_gain_m3|)`). Production's `water_conservation_relative_tolerance` is `core.config.default_mass_balance_relative_tolerance = 1.0e-9` (confirmed in `ecosys-ng/src/core/config.zig:7`), which matches the filing's own back-computed `limit_m3/before_m3 ~ 1e-9` exactly.

**Classification against the task's three hypotheses:**

- **(a) NOT a carrier-basis sibling of issue-065.** This check compares raw water-equivalent volumes end to end; there is no concentration<->amount round trip against a live-vs-dry-reference water carrier anywhere in its own math or in the two upstream terms it consumes (`vapor_internal_gain_m3` is a pure face-flux sum in `hydrology`; `boundary_gain_m3`/`post_phase_external_water_change_m3` is `ground_vapor_exchange.accepted().water_change_m3`, itself an exact `vapor_water_change_m3 + liquid_water_change_m3` sum, confirmed by direct read of `ground_vapor_exchange.zig:116,65`). Confirms the filing's own framing.
- **(b) A mechanism specific to the water pipeline, not the Richards solver narrowly.** The Richards (Newton) solve itself has its own internal per-layer closure check (`validatePerLayerRichardsWaterClosure`, `heat_step.zig:1720`) that already passed for this hour (the run reached the *later*, broader `validatePerLayerPostRichardsTotalWaterClosure` check at all). The failure sits in the bookkeeping that spans the *later* stages of the same hourly transaction -- vapor transport, phase change, and specifically the `postPhasePreHeat` ground-surface vapor/liquid exchange hook -- not inside the Richards solver's own Newton iteration.
- **(c) Same degenerate layer-0-goes-dry state, first time it has tripped a water-quantity-itself check, and a genuinely different flavor than issue-065's chain.** Confirmed: `before_m3` at cell 0/layer 0 is already only `1.04e-7 m3` (essentially fully desiccated) *before* this check's window even starts, matching hour 2894/2895's now-familiar degenerate state. What is new is the arithmetic shape of the failure: `vapor_internal_gain_m3` (`+8.989e-6 m3`, vapor moving up into layer 0 from below) and `boundary_gain_m3` (`-9.093e-6 m3`, surface evaporation removing water) are each ~87x *larger* than the layer's own remaining water content, and nearly cancel to reproduce that tiny remaining content. The check's own `residual_m3` (`5.786e-16`) is the leftover mismatch of that near-total cancellation, and it is a genuine (if tiny) numerical discrepancy -- `activity_scale_m3` in this call is computed from `max(before_m3, after_m3, expected_gain_m3)` (the *already-cancelled*, ~1e-7-scale quantities), not from the *raw* ~9e-6-scale terms (`vapor_internal_gain_m3[layer]`, `boundary_gain_m3`) that were actually subtracted to produce `expected_gain_m3` in `heat_step.zig:3483`. The `representation_floor_m3` this produces (`4096*eps*1.04e-7 ~ 9.5e-20`) is ~6 million times smaller than the observed residual, so it is not merely "too tight by a small margin" -- the floor's scale basis is structurally wrong for a near-total-cancellation case.

**Why a naive fix (widen the floor to use the raw ~9e-6 terms) is NOT obviously sufficient, and was deliberately not applied.** Recomputing `representation_floor_m3` from `max(before_m3, after_m3, expected_gain_m3, |vapor_internal_gain_m3[layer]|, |boundary_gain_m3|)` (~9.09e-6) with the same `4096*eps` multiplier gives `~8.3e-18` -- still ~70x smaller than the observed `5.79e-16` residual. Pure IEEE double-precision roundoff of subtracting two ~9e-6-scale numbers cannot produce a `5.79e-16`-scale error; something looser than machine epsilon is the real source. The leading candidate, not yet confirmed: the vapor-transport solve (`vapor_solver.solveAndBindTransportFaces`, whose face fluxes become `vapor_internal_gain_m3`) converges to `NonlinearTolerance.water_volume_m3` (default `1.0e-13` absolute, `ecosys-ng/src/core/config.zig:103-104`) or a similarly scaled Newton residual criterion, not to machine epsilon -- i.e. the ~9e-6-scale flux terms feeding this closure check may themselves carry ~1e-13-to-1e-16-scale solver-convergence slack that is real (not IEEE roundoff) and is not currently surfaced to this check as an `upstream_arithmetic_roundoff_allowance`. This has NOT been confirmed by tracing the vapor solver's actual accepted residual at this hour/cell/layer -- doing so is the correct next step, not a guess at the floor formula.

**Materiality/reachability.** Not hour-2895-specific. The structural condition -- a layer's total water decaying to a magnitude much smaller than the vapor-transport and surface-exchange fluxes simultaneously passing through it -- recurs by construction during any dry-down episode and is not unique to day 121/hour 2895; this exact cell/layer has already been the site of every issue in the hour-2894/2895 chain (issue-060 through issue-066, issue-065's eighteen passes). Expect this class of failure to resurface at other hours/layers once this one is fixed, unless the fix addresses the general representation-floor/provenance gap rather than this one hour's numbers.

Widening `representation_floor_m3` by formula alone does not close the gap (see above) and widening `water_conservation_relative_tolerance` globally would be a forbidden blanket tolerance weakening. No `zig build`/test/run was executed this pass (read-only source analysis only); the diagnosis budget's three-experiment counter is therefore still unspent for this issue -- the first bounded experiment should be a diagnostic trace (not a fix attempt) of the vapor solver's own accepted residual for cell 0/layer 0 at hour 2895, matching the same evidence discipline already used for issue-060 through issue-065.

## Confirmation, fix, and validation (2026-09-20, later pass)

**Experiment 1 -- locate and confirm the vapor solver's own accepted tolerance.** Read `ecosys-ng/src/soil/gas/vapor_solver.zig`'s `Options`/`scaledNorm`/`residualAt`: the solve accepts convergence per cell (= per layer, flattened) when `|residual[layer]| <= absolute_tolerance_m3 + relative_tolerance*|current[layer]|`, where `current[layer]` is exactly `grid.water_vapor_volume_m3[layer]` -- the same physical quantity, same units, as the term this closure check consumes as `vapor_internal_gain_m3`. Traced production's actual values into this call (`heat_step.zig`'s `advanceMappedDeferred` -> `hourly_heat_water_solute.zig:12672`): `.water_absolute_tolerance_m3 = context.config.nonlinear_tolerance.water_volume_m3`, `.nonlinear_relative_tolerance = context.config.nonlinear_tolerance.relative` -- i.e. `core_config.NonlinearTolerance.fromLegacy(...)`'s `water_volume_m3 = absolute_tolerance*1e-2` and `.relative = relative_tolerance` (the deck's own legacy `runtime` record), NOT the bare struct defaults. This needed empirical confirmation, not just static reading, because a *different* call site (`ecosys_ng.zig:3998`, unrelated litter-salt-ingress code) uses `physical_tolerance.water_volume_m3` (default `1e-14`) for a same-named field, and it was not safe to assume which one applied here without checking the deck's actual runtime record.

**Experiment 2 -- direct instrumentation, one fresh-from-hour-1 run.** Added a narrowly-gated diagnostic (`vapor_solver.Options.diagnostic_trace_layer_index`, threaded through `heat_step.MappedOptions.diagnostic_vapor_layer_index`, set only when `context.executed_weather_hours` is in a 3-hour window around 2,895 in `hourly_heat_water_solute.zig`) that logs the vapor solver's own accepted per-layer residual and tolerance band at the moment its stopping criterion is satisfied. Two false starts before a clean measurement, both disclosed honestly: (a) an initial run used `--verbose-diagnostics`, which also re-enables a large volume of unrelated high-frequency logging elsewhere in this stage and made the run prohibitively slow (the diagnostic gate was rewritten to not depend on that global flag); (b) a rebuild was accidentally done without `-Doptimize=ReleaseFast`, defaulting to a Debug build whose default `std.log` level is `.debug` -- this silently re-enabled an unrelated, pre-existing, already-gated-but-defaulted-on debug trace (`reaction_solve.zig`'s `SOLUTE full-network QR pivot norms`, gated by `reaction_diagnostic_control.isEnabled()`, which defaults to `true`) on every SOLUTE Newton iteration, flooding the log (>1 GB) and, combined with unoptimized codegen, causing a severe slowdown -- caught, explained, and corrected (rebuilt with `-Doptimize=ReleaseFast`) before drawing any conclusion from it. **Result: CONFIRMED.** Production's `water_absolute_tolerance_m3` is exactly `1e-13` (matching the prior pass's cited value) and `relative_tolerance` is `1e-8`. The vapor solver's own accepted residual for this layer (`5.786488638508808e-16`) matches the closure check's own residual (`5.786492873673544e-16`) to ~7 significant figures -- the closure check's "mismatch" *is* (to high precision) the vapor solver's own already-tolerated Newton residual, carried through unchanged, not a new or independent error. That residual sits comfortably inside the solver's own accepted band (`1.899e-13`), which is ~1,825x the closure check's own pure-relative `limit_m3` (`1.04e-16`) -- exactly the mismatch-of-provenance the prior pass predicted.

**Fix implemented.** `validatePerLayerPostRichardsTotalWaterClosure` (`ecosys-ng/src/soil/water/heat_step.zig`) now takes two additional parameters, `vapor_solver_absolute_tolerance_m3`/`vapor_solver_relative_tolerance`, and adds `vapor_solver_absolute_tolerance_m3 + vapor_solver_relative_tolerance*|vapor_internal_gain_m3[layer]|` into the existing `upstream_arithmetic_roundoff_allowance` term (alongside the pre-existing `representation_floor_m3` IEEE-roundoff term). The production call site (`heat_step.zig`'s call around line 2104) passes `inputs.vapor_options.absolute_tolerance_m3, inputs.vapor_options.relative_tolerance` -- the *exact same* tolerance values already fed to the vapor solver itself at `heat_step.zig:1550`. Every pre-existing test call site was updated to pass `0, 0`, preserving their exact prior behavior unchanged.

**Why this is not the forbidden "widen a tolerance to manufacture a pass."** `scoped_conservation.Tolerance.upstream_arithmetic_roundoff_allowance` is a pre-existing, purpose-built field (see `scoped_conservation.zig`'s own doc comment: "provenance-derived forward-error bound from arithmetic performed upstream of this final closure evaluation... reported separately from, and never changes, the configured physical tolerance"). This fix populates that existing field with a value drawn from a *different, already-tested, already-accepted* authoritative source (the vapor solver's own configured Newton convergence criterion) -- it does not touch `water_conservation_relative_tolerance`, `water_conservation_absolute_tolerance_m`, or any other physical/scientific acceptance criterion. It is the same "two parts of the code should agree on the same authoritative value" class of fix as this chain's other recent fixes (e.g. issue-065's carrier-basis fix), not a new slack invented for this one check. The check still rejects any residual not explained by that solver's own declared tolerance -- proven by regression test part (c) below.

**Regression test.** `ecosys-ng/src/soil/water/heat_step.zig`, test `"post-Richards vapor water gate accounts for the vapor solver's own accepted convergence slack without becoming toothless"`, using the *exact* recorded hour-2895 before/after/flux/boundary values: (a) the OLD pure-relative check (`vapor_solver_*_tolerance=0,0`) rejects this exact case, reproducing the original bug; (b) the NEW hybrid check, given the vapor solver's real production tolerances (`1e-13`, `1e-8`), accepts it; (c) the NEW hybrid check still rejects a genuine violation (an injected extra `1e-11` m3, ~50x the solver's own accepted band) -- proving the fix is not toothless. Verified via targeted `zig test src/module_index.zig --test-filter "..."` runs (`"post-Richards"`, `"closure"`, `"heat_step"`, `"vapor"`), not the full suite; all passed except one pre-existing, unrelated failure (`io.output.production_integration_test.zig`'s `"hourly soil water output includes accepted ground evaporation"`, a source-text-scan test against `ecosys_ng.zig`, a file this fix never touched -- confirmed pre-existing and out of scope, not caused by or fixed in this pass).
