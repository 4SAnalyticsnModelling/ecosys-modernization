# Scientific evidence and history

Current workflow authority is [the leader protocol](../.agent/protocol.md). One leader owns
decisions, verification and publication; safe supporting sub-agents/swarms are optional.
There are no adversarial reviewer roles or reviewer approval gates.

Older issues, handoffs, reviews, release records and run artifacts retain historical actor
labels and pending-review language as provenance. They do not impose current role assignments,
reviewer sign-offs, approval queues or fresh-context requirements. The leader evaluates their
scientific claims from raw evidence and resolves remaining work. Historical missing verification
still needs real tests or derivation; it does not require another reviewer.

Preserve original receipts and observations. A historical PASS, CONFIRMED or approval label
does not establish current qualification. Current source/binding hashes, complete scientific
coverage and the protocol's evidence gates determine acceptance.
